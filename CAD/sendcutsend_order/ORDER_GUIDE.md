## Notes

- Files are at 1:1 scale in millimeters
- All parts are on layer 0
- No text or dimensions included - just cut paths
- Upload files one at a time (SendCutSend doesn't accept ZIP files)

## After Cutting

The 9mm parts need to be glued in pairs to create 18mm thickness:
1. Match identical parts
2. Apply wood glue evenly
3. Clamp and let dry 24 hours
4. Sand edges flush if needed
