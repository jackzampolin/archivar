Hi,

this is not a comprehensive explanation on how to build an Archivar, its just the raw files we used for producing the scanners for those who want to build their own and have time to dig through it.

If you need any parts (cameras, lenses, wooden parts etc.) you can try contacting us via email or via the forum. We are open to selling them, it may however take some time to get a reply as we have lots of other tasks on our dish.

Under "Hardware" you'll find some hints what parts you need.
Under "CAD" you'll find 2 Versions of the main structure:
LE) 18mm_plywood to cut all parts from 18mm plywood
LE) 8plus10mm if you want to use 2 thinner sheets and sandwich them (eg. If your machine can't handle 18mm thick sheets)
Under "Logo" you'll find the Logo a friend of ours created for the scanner. If you can integrate in your build, that would be awesome.
